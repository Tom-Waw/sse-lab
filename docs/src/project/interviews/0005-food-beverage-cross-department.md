# Interview Guide 0005: Food & Beverage / Cross-Department Operations

## Purpose

This interview explores food and beverage, bar, and cross-department coordination.

The goal is not to expand `sse-lab` into a full food and beverage system. The goal is to understand coordination patterns, handoffs, guest requests, and operational dependencies that may influence scope boundaries or future workflow design.

This interview should help identify which cross-department concerns are relevant to the hospitality operations domain and which should remain out of scope.

## Focus Areas

- Cross-department coordination
- Guest requests
- Handoffs between operational areas
- Shared operational visibility
- Scope boundaries
- Future integration ideas

## Questions

### Workflow

- What kinds of operational coordination happen between departments?
- Which departments depend on information from each other?
- What kinds of guest requests require cross-department communication?
- Which handoffs are common during a shift?

### Food & Beverage / Bar

- At a high level, what operational information matters in food and beverage or bar work?
- Which tasks or requests need coordination with front office or management?
- What kinds of delays or misunderstandings can affect guest experience?
- Which information is useful across departments?

### Cross-Department Coordination

- Where do handoffs commonly break down?
- Which information is often missing or delayed?
- What kinds of issues require escalation?
- Which operational problems are caused by unclear ownership?

### Scope Boundaries

- Which food and beverage concerns are clearly outside the initial project scope?
- Which cross-department patterns are still useful for the hotel operations model?
- Which future integrations might be interesting but should not be built now?

## Follow-up Prompts

- Is this a general coordination pattern or specific to one workplace?
- Does this belong in the core system or only as future context?
- Which role owns this problem?
- What information would make the handoff clearer?
- Can this be generalized without describing a specific employer process?

## Expected Insights

This interview may produce generalized findings about:

- cross-department handoffs;
- escalation paths;
- guest request coordination;
- scope boundaries;
- future integration candidates;
- operational visibility beyond front office and housekeeping.
